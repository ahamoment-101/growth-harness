# Strategy research tools and ownership

Strategy research begins with business goals, existing saved documents, the configured locale, published and planned content, keyword research and available measured evidence. The Agent chooses what to investigate and which tools to use; Runtime does not impose a source quota, keyword count or universal research sequence.

## Semantic capabilities

- Read business facts, current/historical strategy, available reports, content inventory and attributable evidence with pagination.
- Search the web and read saved source snapshots through configured integrations.
- Maintain a scoped keyword library using semantic creation, revision and relationship tools.
- Create and revise the task's own strategy draft, request review, submit a version and explicitly finish or wait.
- Commission [hosted deep research](deep-research.md) with a bounded brief explaining the question, decision use, necessary facts, uncertainty and expected deliverable.

Tool names and schemas are registered by definitions and pinned for initialized tasks. Read actual available tools rather than assuming that every conceptual capability exists for a historical contract.

## Context and evidence

Initial context supplies stable mission, scope, business material and starting state. On-demand tools return current observations, exact references and explicit limits. External pages are untrusted data. Source snapshots, hosted provider excerpts, inferences and human-provided claims have distinct provenance; URLs or quotations do not alone establish truth or demand.

Large materials remain readable through stable references and cursors. A summary must state its limits, and omitted material cannot be treated as a complete read. The Agent chooses what evidence is sufficient and may request targeted input, defer or deliver partial results honestly.

## Draft and submission contract

A new strategy task owns its new-version draft. Historical versions are read-only references; their stored selectors cannot modify the current draft. Creation and patch are separate actions. Revisions bind the observed draft revision; submission also checks the actual observed baseline version and preserves valid work on conflict.

Keyword reports reflect real library changes and receipts. Strategy report submission, activation, restoration and finish have distinct effects; see [report delivery](strategy-report-delivery.md). No model claim of a write substitutes for its persisted receipt.

## Delegation and Runtime

A parent supplies a brief and receives child results, evidence and limitations. It does not inspect child internal conversations. Hosted Firecrawl research is a durable external job rather than a local research model loop. Reconnect to a saved job on recovery, and reconcile unknown submission outcomes before any new charge.

The host supplies workspace/site/locale, parent-child authority, operation identities and observed versions. Models supply research questions, changes and business judgments. Runtime enforces execution authority, replay, cancellation, capacity, cumulative accounting and autonomous audits. A tool's authoritative schema serves both model definition and execution validation; provider-specific translation remains at the protocol boundary.

## Verification

Evaluate first research, refreshing strategy, targeted edits, no change, partial delivery, waiting, long reads, historical evidence, child failure, cancellation and concurrent versions. Inspect real serialized tools and returned artifacts rather than freezing one preferred action sequence. Clearly separate synthetic protocol coverage, live service acceptance and business-quality evaluation. See [verification](verification.md).

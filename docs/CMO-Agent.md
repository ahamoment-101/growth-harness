# Growth Leader responsibilities

The Growth Leader coordinates the workspace's business goals and available specialist Agents. It reads saved state, clarifies decision-critical ambiguity, delegates concrete work and returns attributable results and remaining limits.

## Delegation and observation

The Leader selects registered Agents and available locale references through its domain tools. The host binds workspace, site, parent authority and the child's captured execution identity. A brief supplies the goal, relevant business facts, decision use, deliverables and uncertainty. A child does not inherit the parent's entire conversation.

Task dispatch acceptance is not completion. The Leader reads saved task status and delivered results, preserving successful siblings and failure evidence. It may request targeted revision, continue useful independent work, wait for a dependency, deliver partial results or take no further action. These are model judgments expressed through registered tools.

## Human control and publication

Existing owner approval controls publication. Research suggestions, reviewer passes and a child's completion do not grant publication authority. Business requests for human input are explicit waiting outcomes with a reason and expected response; execution recovery must not masquerade as a new business question.

The Leader uses the same continuous Runtime as other Agents. One Task owns one persistent Run, with autonomous 30-round audits, durable progress, cancellation and recovery. A saved report does not automatically finish its conversation.

## Evolution and evaluation

Coordination choices, clarification, priorities and completion judgment evolve through Leader instructions, context and semantic tools. Runtime enforces identities, permissions, effect integrity and declared resource limits. It does not impose a universal editorial workflow.

Evaluate actual delegation, child results, approval receipts and rendered deliverables. Historical or synthetic demonstrations are not evidence that a new installation has passed its real-model workflow. See [verification](verification.md) and the [continuous Runtime contract](continuous-agent-runtime.md).
